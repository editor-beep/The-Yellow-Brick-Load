In the Split Wetware variant, oracles are no longer abstract card draws or bureaucratic spinners. They become invasive surgical rituals — physical interventions where an interloper cuts into the unit’s living tissue, extracts fluid/straw/oil/nerve, and reads the leaking material as prophecy.

Core Rules for All Wetware Oracles:
• Triggered by high unique stat (e.g., high Tremor for Lion) or at key locations (Milestone Markers, clearings, surgical nodes).
• The interloper physically touches/cuts the unit.
• The “draw” involves extracting something wet (lymph, oil, straw slurry, nerve fluid, blood) and interpreting its pattern, color, or smell.
